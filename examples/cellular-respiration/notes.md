# Cellular respiration — lecture notes

Cellular respiration is how cells turn the energy stored in glucose into ATP, the molecule cells use to pay for work.
Overall: glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water + ATP. It happens in three main stages, plus a backup route when
oxygen runs out.

## 1. Glycolysis
- Happens in the **cytoplasm**. Does **not** need oxygen.
- One glucose (6 carbons) is split into **two pyruvate** molecules (3 carbons each).
- The cell first **invests 2 ATP**, then **makes 4 ATP**, so the **net gain is 2 ATP**.
- It also makes **2 NADH**, which carry high-energy electrons to the electron transport chain.

## 2. The Krebs cycle (citric acid cycle)
- Happens in the **mitochondrial matrix**.
- Each pyruvate is first turned into **acetyl-CoA** (2 carbons), releasing one CO₂.
- Acetyl-CoA joins **oxaloacetate** (4 carbons) to form **citrate** (6 carbons). Citrate is broken back down to
  oxaloacetate, so the cycle can start again.
- Each turn releases **2 CO₂** and makes **3 NADH, 1 FADH₂ and 1 ATP**.
- The cycle turns **twice per glucose** (one turn for each pyruvate).

## 3. Electron transport chain and ATP synthase
- Happens on the **inner mitochondrial membrane**.
- NADH and FADH₂ drop off **electrons**, which are passed along a chain of proteins (complexes I, III and IV).
- The energy released **pumps H⁺ ions** from the matrix into the space between the membranes, building up a
  concentration gradient.
- H⁺ flows back into the matrix through **ATP synthase**, which spins and makes ATP. This makes **about 26–28 ATP**,
  most of the cell's total.
- **Oxygen is the final electron acceptor.** It picks up the electrons and H⁺ and forms **water**. Without oxygen
  the chain backs up and stops.

## Totals
- With oxygen (aerobic respiration): about **30–32 ATP per glucose**.

## 4. Without oxygen: fermentation
- If there isn't enough oxygen, pyruvate goes to **fermentation** instead of the Krebs cycle.
- In **human muscle cells**, pyruvate becomes **lactate** (lactic acid). In **yeast**, it becomes **ethanol and CO₂**
  (why bread rises).
- Fermentation makes **no extra ATP**. Its job is to **regenerate NAD⁺**, so glycolysis can keep running and keep
  making its 2 ATP.
