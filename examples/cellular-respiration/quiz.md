# Recall quiz — cellular respiration (give 2–3 days after studying, no notes)

1. Where in the cell does glycolysis happen?
2. Glycolysis splits glucose into two molecules of what? How many carbons does each have?
3. What is the net ATP gain from glycolysis?
4. Where does the Krebs cycle take place?
5. Acetyl-CoA combines with which molecule, and what does it form?
6. How many times does the Krebs cycle turn per glucose?
7. What gas is released by the Krebs cycle?
8. What does the electron transport chain pump across the membrane, and what enzyme makes ATP when it flows back?
9. What is the final electron acceptor, and what does it become?
10. What do human muscles make during fermentation, and why does fermentation happen at all?

## Answer key (1 point each; half points allowed for 2-part answers)
1. Cytoplasm
2. Pyruvate; 3 carbons each
3. 2 ATP (4 made, 2 used)
4. Mitochondrial matrix
5. Oxaloacetate; citrate
6. Twice
7. Carbon dioxide (CO₂)
8. H⁺ ions (protons); ATP synthase
9. Oxygen; water
10. Lactate (lactic acid); to regenerate NAD⁺ so glycolysis can keep making ATP
